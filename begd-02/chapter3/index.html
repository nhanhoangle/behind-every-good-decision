Seven lenses for a question

Each methodology frames evidence differently. Use one or combine them as an investigation becomes more specific.

01Aggregate analysis

Describe a group or compare its parts.

02Correlation analysis

Find relationships between variables.

03Trends analysis

Track how performance changes over time.

04Sizing & estimation

Estimate an opportunity when complete data is missing.

05Predictive / time series

Use the past to anticipate likely outcomes.

06Segmentation

Group similar customers, products, or situations.

07Customer life cycle

Examine stages from awareness to retention.

How to choose

Match the method to the question. “What happened?” often calls for aggregate or trends analysis. “What could happen?” may need prediction. “For whom?” frequently benefits from segmentation.

One question, several lenses

A retailer wanting more repeat orders could compare customer groups, track repeat behavior over time, build segments, and identify the stage where customers disengage. Each lens makes the recommendation sharper.

Start simply

Simple methods are powerful because they are explainable, quick to test, and easy to connect to a decision. Escalate complexity only when the decision needs more precision.

Takeaway

The best methodology is not the most sophisticated one. It is the one that gives the decision-maker a reliable next move.
